Research Projects

Comprehensive Simulation Testing Platform of Navigation Satellite Based on BD32024 – 2025

Research Assistant · Al-Farabi Kazakh National University & Northwestern Polytechnical University

A simulation platform for BeiDou-3 navigation satellites, combining constellation generation, satellite visibility modeling, 2D and 3D graphic display, constellation analysis (PDOP and satellite number curves) and positioning simulation. Built with VC++ and MATLAB.

Scientific adviser: Xuanmin Lu, PhD, Associate Professor

Safe Taxi Application2024

Research Adviser · Al-Farabi Kazakh National University

A real-time system that monitors taxi and driver behavior using a camera and GPS, built with Python, Django, TensorFlow and OpenCV, with a dashboard for trip analysis and safety evaluation.

Detection accuracy: drowsiness 91.5%, phone usage 90.1%, aggressive driving 89.9%, speed violation 88.2%, route deviation 87.6%.

Bachelor's student team: Ahmad Shah Yawar, Hasibullah Badghisi, Hekmatullah Suha, Merwais Mohebi
